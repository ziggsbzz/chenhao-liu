I am an Assistant Professor at the School of Finance, Shanghai University of Finance and Economics (SUFE). My research interests lie in development economics, public economics, and political economy. Currently, my research focuses on the topic of institution. Drawing on theoretical insights from public, development, and international economics, I employ novel datasets and empirical designs to understand the evolution of institution building, the impacts of institutional design, and the interaction between informal and formal institutions.

I received a Ph.D. in Economics from the Guanghua School of Management, Peking University in 2025; an M.A. (all but degree) in Environmental Science from the College of Environmental Sciences and Engineering, Peking University in 2022; and a B.S. in Economics from the School of Economics, Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2020.
